\begin{frame}{Style of Lecture}
The vision of designing and building gateways, nodes and web applications has a major implication on the lecture style:
\begin{itemize}
    \item It will \alert{not be a classical lecture} but more of a \alert{workshop}.
    \item I will \alert{not lecture a lot}, but sometimes i will.
    \item I will be \alert{your coach} during the workshop
    \begin{itemize}
        \item advising on technologies
        \item advising on hardware and software
        \item advising on ESP-32 and Raspberry Pi
        \item advising on MQTT
        \item \ldots
    \end{itemize}
    \item You will do \alert{research about topics} related and necessary \alert{for the project}.
    \item You will \alert{design and build the hardware} for the LoRaWAN nodes.
    \item You will \alert{develop the protocol} used to transmit the measurements to The Things Network.
    \item You will \alert{design and implement the Web applications} used to administrate the gateways and nodes.
    \item You will \alert{design and implement} the Frontend Web application for \alert{data access} and \alert{data analysis}.
    \item \ldots
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
