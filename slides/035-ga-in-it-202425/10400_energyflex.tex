\section[withsectionpage]{EnergyFlex -- Use Electricity When It Is Green}

\begin{frame}{EnergyFlex -- Project Vision}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Renewable generation and electricity demand do not always occur at the same time.
    \item Some consumption is \textbf{critical and immediate}, while other loads can be shifted in time.
    \item Examples of flexible loads include water pumping, charging, heating, cooling and other deferrable community services.
    \item Using flexible loads when renewable energy is abundant increases local renewable-energy utilization.
\end{itemize}

\begin{block}{Project Goal}
Demonstrate how IoT can automatically adapt electricity consumption to renewable-energy availability without compromising critical loads.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
