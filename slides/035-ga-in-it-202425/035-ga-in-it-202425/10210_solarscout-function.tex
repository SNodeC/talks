\begin{frame}{SolarScout -- What the Project Will Do}
\small
\begin{itemize}
    \item Measure available solar radiation or the output of a small reference PV module.
    \item Measure environmental parameters influencing solar yield, especially ambient and panel temperature.
    \item Determine panel orientation and inclination and record the exact geographic location.
    \item Periodically transmit measurements through LoRaWAN and store them centrally with time and location.
    \item Compare different sites, orientations and shading situations.
    \item Present measurements and estimated solar potential in a web-based geographic view.
    \item Produce a dataset that can later support prediction and site-selection work in the AI and Data Science modules.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
