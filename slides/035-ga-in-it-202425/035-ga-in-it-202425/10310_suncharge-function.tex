\begin{frame}{SunCharge -- What the Project Will Do}
\small
\begin{itemize}
    \item Generate energy with a small photovoltaic module and store it in a low-voltage battery system.
    \item Continuously measure solar production, battery charging/discharging power, connected load and relevant temperatures.
    \item Estimate whether sufficient renewable or stored energy is available for charging.
    \item Implement simple policies such as \textbf{charge now, delay a non-critical load, protect a low battery, or limit/disconnect a load}.
    \item Record energy flows and charging sessions for later analysis.
    \item Show current system status and historical usage in a web dashboard.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
