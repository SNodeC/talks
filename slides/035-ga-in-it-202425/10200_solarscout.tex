\section[nosectionpage]{SolarScout -- IoT Solar-Potential Mapping Network}

\begin{frame}{SolarScout -- Project Vision}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Solar installations are only useful if their location and orientation provide sufficient energy.
    \item Solar potential can vary significantly because of \textbf{orientation, inclination, shading and local environmental conditions}.
    \item Portable IoT measurement stations can collect comparable measurements at different locations.
    \item Combining measurements with geographic position makes solar potential visible spatially.
\end{itemize}

\begin{block}{Project Goal}
Build a distributed measurement system that helps identify good locations and configurations for future photovoltaic installations.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
