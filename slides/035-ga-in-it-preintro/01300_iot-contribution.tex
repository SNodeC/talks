\section[nosectionpage]{What Can IoT Contribute?}

\begin{frame}{From Research Question to an IoT System}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Build \textbf{autonomous floating measurement nodes} that can operate on the lake.
    \item Measure water properties continuously and attach \textbf{position and time} to every measurement.
    \item Use \textbf{LoRaWAN} to move data from distributed nodes to gateways around the lake.
    \item Integrate the gateways into \textbf{The Things Network}.
    \item Forward the measurements to a backend and make them available through a \textbf{web application}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{block}{The Big Idea}
Turn a difficult-to-observe ecological process into a continuous, distributed stream of real-world data.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
