\begin{frame}{International Minor Global Acting in IT}{One Semester -- Five Countries -- One Project}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{One semester} of international study and collaboration.
    \item \textbf{Five countries, five universities and five ICT specializations} contribute to one common project.
    \item The modules are \textbf{not isolated courses}: every stop adds another capability to the same stakeholder-driven challenge.
    \item The programme combines \textbf{intercultural experience}, \textbf{international teamwork} and \textbf{ICT with positive societal or environmental impact}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{block}{The Hagenberg Contribution}
In Hagenberg, the project meets the physical world: sensing, connectivity, data acquisition and actuation become a working IoT system.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
