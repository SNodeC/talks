\part[nopartpage]{How the Programme Works}

\begin{frame}{One Project Across a Whole Semester}{30 ECTS -- Learning Across Borders}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{.31\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Prepare Together}
Online start-up: meet the team, understand the shared challenge and prepare for working across borders.
\end{block}
\column{.34\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Build Across Five Countries}
Five teaching blocks contribute complementary ICT skills to the same project. Each location adds a new perspective.
\end{block}
\column{.31\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Bring It Together}
Complete the project work and portfolio, reflect on the experience and make the learning visible.
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\vspace{1em}
\begin{alertblock}{A Shared Thread}
The project connects the semester: teams carry their questions, decisions and results from one partner institution to the next.
\end{alertblock}
