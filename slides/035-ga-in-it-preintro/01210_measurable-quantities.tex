\begin{frame}{From Biology to Measurable Quantities}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nutrients:} nitrate, ammonium and phosphate
    \item \textbf{Conductivity / TDS:} indicators of ionic load and inflows
    \item \textbf{pH and temperature:} influence biological activity and nutrient availability
    \item \textbf{Turbidity:} indicates light penetration and suspended material
    \item \textbf{Dissolved oxygen:} important ecosystem-health indicator beneath dense mats
    \item \textbf{Location and time:} needed to understand where and when conditions change
\end{itemize}
\begin{alertblock}{Technology Comes After the Research Question}
The project did not start with an ESP32 or LoRaWAN. It started with the question: \textbf{what do researchers need to know?}
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
