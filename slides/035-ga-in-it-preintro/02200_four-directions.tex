\section[nosectionpage]{Candidate Project Directions}

\begin{frame}{Four Directions We Are Exploring}
\begin{description}[SolarScout]
    \item[GreenShare] A \textbf{smart renewable-energy community}: coordinate production, consumption, storage and flexible loads.
    \item[SolarScout] A \textbf{solar-potential mapping network}: measure where and how solar generation works best.
    \item[SunCharge] A \textbf{renewable-aware charging station}: manage solar generation, storage and useful charging services.
    \item[EnergyFlex] \textbf{Use electricity when it is green}: move flexible demand into periods of renewable surplus.
\end{description}
\begin{alertblock}{These Are Draft Directions}
They show the design space. They are not four promises that all four projects will be built.
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
