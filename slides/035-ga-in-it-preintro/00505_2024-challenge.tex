\begin{frame}{2024: Understanding Invasive Species}{Start with the Problem -- Then Ask What Technology Can Contribute}
\begin{block}{The Overall Theme}
Invasive species were the shared International Minor topic in both \textbf{2024 and 2025}.
\end{block}
\begin{itemize}
\item Water hyacinths at Hartbeespoort Dam provide a concrete case: an ecological problem with consequences for people and the environment.
\item First, understand the stakeholders, the biology and the conditions that drive the infestation.
\item Then ask which observations and measurements could help researchers understand what is happening.
\end{itemize}
\begin{alertblock}{One Theme Across Two Years}
In 2024, students built a floating measurement system. We will follow the path from the ecological problem to the buoy and workshop, then revisit the same overall theme in 2025.
\end{alertblock}
