\section[nosectionpage]{This Is How the Hagenberg Workshop Works}

\begin{frame}{Three Intense Weeks in Hagenberg}{Not a Classical Lecture}
\begin{description}[You Will]
    \item[I will] act mainly as your \textbf{coach}: teach where needed, advise on technology and help teams unblock problems.
    \item[You will]
    \begin{itemize}
        \item research what the project actually needs,
        \item make technical decisions,
        \item design and build hardware,
        \item program nodes and backend software,
        \item integrate communication, data and web applications,
        \item test, fail, fix and integrate again,
        \item finish with a \textbf{working demonstration}.
    \end{itemize}
\end{description}
\begin{block}{The Learning Model}
You do not just learn about an IoT system -- you experience what it takes to build one as an international team.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
