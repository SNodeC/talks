\begin{frame}{From a Learning Idea to an Educational Device}{Understanding Microgrids Without Prior Knowledge of Electricity}
Your gamified learning idea and planned physical gift packages inspired a new direction: an educational device for people with little or no prior knowledge of electricity, generation or storage.
