\section[nosectionpage]{First Understand the Problem}

\begin{frame}{What Drives the Mats?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nutrients:} nitrogen and phosphorus strongly influence growth and biomass.
    \item \textbf{Hydrodynamics:} sheltered zones favour establishment; wind moves and concentrates mats.
    \item \textbf{Climate and seasonality:} warm temperatures and high irradiance accelerate growth.
    \item \textbf{Disturbances and interventions:} storms, floods, removal and biological control change distribution.
\end{itemize}
\begin{block}{The Important Step}
Before choosing sensors or communication technology, we first need to understand \textbf{which physical processes matter}.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
