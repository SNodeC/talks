\section{Install XCA for Certificate Management}
\withsectionpage

\begin{frame}{Install \alert{XCA} on your PC for Certificate Management}
  \alert{XCA} is an \alert{oben source} and \alert{versatile} utility for \alert{managing} one or more \alert{CA}s. There are \alert{many more tools} for CA management \alert{out in the web} but I find \alert{XCA intuitiv} and \alert{feature} rich compared to others. It is available for \alert{all relevant operating systems}.
\begin{exampleblock}{Tasks (individually): Install XCA}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \alert{Download} XCA from its website \href{https://hohnstaedt.de/xca/}{\beamergotobutton{https://hohnstaedt.de/xca/}} and \alert{install} it.
    \item \alert{Start} XCA.
  \end{enumerate}
  \end{exampleblock}
\end{frame}
