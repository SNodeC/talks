\part{Message Queuing and Telemetry Transport (MQTT)}

\begin{frame}{What is MQTT?}

  \centering\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{images/MQTT}%
  \vspace{2em}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{MQTT} is a lightweight \textbf{publish/subscribe} messaging protocol.
    \item Designed for \textbf{small devices} and \textbf{unreliable/low-bandwidth} networks (IoT).
    \item Runs typically over \textbf{TCP/IP}; clients connect to a \textbf{broker} (server).
    \item Messages are sent to \textbf{topics} (like channel names), not directly to other clients.
  \end{itemize}

  \vspace{1ex}
  \footnotesize
  Key idea: \textbf{decouple} data producers (sensors) from data consumers (apps/dashboards).
\end{frame}
