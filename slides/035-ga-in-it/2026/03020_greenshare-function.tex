\begin{frame}{GreenShare -- What the Project Will Do}
\small
\begin{itemize}
    \item Measure electrical production and consumption at several simulated houses or community facilities.
    \item Represent photovoltaic generation using small real PV modules and/or controllable simulated generation.
    \item Determine the current energy balance of each participant: \textbf{producer, consumer or balanced}.
    \item Calculate the overall community energy balance and identify renewable-energy surplus.
    \item Match available surplus with flexible demand and automatically switch selected loads.
    \item Store measurements and control decisions for later analysis.
    \item Present current and historical production, consumption and energy use in a web application.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
