\begin{frame}{EnergyFlex -- What the Project Will Do}
\small
\begin{itemize}
    \item Measure or receive the current renewable-energy situation.
    \item Monitor the operating state and energy demand of several loads.
    \item Classify loads, for example as \textbf{critical, flexible or deferrable}.
    \item Determine when sufficient renewable energy is available and automatically enable, delay or disable selected flexible loads.
    \item Include process information where useful, such as water level, temperature or charging state.
    \item Store measurements, control decisions and load activity for later analysis.
    \item Compare normal operation with renewable-aware operation and quantify improved use of locally generated energy.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
