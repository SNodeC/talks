\begin{frame}{Properties of Simple Digital and Analog Pins Connections}
\begin{alertblock}{Properties of Simple Digital and Analog Pins Connections}
\begin{description}[Special properties:]
\item [Wires:] Number and function: Usually 2-3: Ground/- and/or Power/VCC/+ and Signal (S)
\item [Speed:] Fast as usually only one bit or one Analog value based on 8-24bits
\item [Max lengths:] Quite long (several meters), but careful with resistance of wire (sometimes also capacitance)
\item [Special properties:] Depends, sometimes too small or too high voltages, PWM is kind of its own bus
\end{description}
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}
