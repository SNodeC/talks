\section{Remember to Write Reflection}

\begin{frame}{Remember to Write Reflection}{One for each lecture and lab $\rightarrow$ personal porfolio!}
\begin{itemize}
    \item What is your \alert{take-away}, what do you want to \alert{remember}, what would be your advice?
\begin{itemize}
    \item Take-away can be \alert{small summary}, but better outstanding points.
    \item What was good?
    \item What was difficult, where did you struggle?
\end{itemize}
\item Was their any good/fun “struggling”/exploration?
\item Any show stoppers – anything where you should reach out to peers or instructors?
\item How was your interaction with peers/instructors?
\begin{itemize}
    \item Who helped you, gave feedback, was it valuable?
    \item Who did you help, gave feedback to?
\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
